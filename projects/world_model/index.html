Action-Conditioned Occupancy World Model for End-to-End Autonomous Planning

Report

Direct end-to-end planners ($\Phi_{\text{E2E}}$) regress future trajectories directly from sensor streams without explicitly modeling 4D environmental dynamics, resulting in weak generalization and degraded safety robustness in complex scenarios. Meanwhile, naively injecting ground-truth ego actions into a coupled forecasting-planning world model introduces ego-status leakage, requiring the system to either detach the planner during pure controllable synthesis or feed back self-predicted trajectories across both training and inference rollouts.

Method Overview

Stage 1 — Conditional BEV Memory Queue ($\Psi_{\mathcal{E}} \rightarrow \Psi_{\mathcal{M}}$)

  • Multi-View Geometric Lifting: The history encoder $\Psi_{\mathcal{E}}$ extracts multi-view spatial geometry from historical camera sequences and projects them into unified latent Bird's-Eye-View grids $\mathbf{B} \in \mathbb{R}^{H_b \times W_b \times C}$.
  • Parameter-Free Adaptive Modulation: Inside the memory queue $\Psi_{\mathcal{M}}$, each historical BEV tensor undergoes parameter-free layer normalization followed by an adaptive affine shift and scale ($\boldsymbol{\gamma}^*, \boldsymbol{\beta}^*$) to enrich latent scene dynamics.
  • Tri-Stream Conditioning: Modulation parameters are regressed from three explicit signals: one-hot encoded voxel-wise semantic predictions passed through convolutions, flattened rigid ego-pose transformation matrices processed via MLPs, and dense 3D backward centripetal flow vectors pointing from each voxel at step $t$ to its 3D instance centroid at step $t-1$.

Stage 2 — Action-Conditioned Autoregressive Decoder ($\Psi_{\mathcal{D}}$)

  • Unified Fourier Action Encoding: Heterogeneous ego-control inputs—planar velocity $(v_x, v_y)$, geometric steering curvature, relative trajectory displacements $(\Delta x, \Delta y)$, and discrete navigation commands—are mapped through sinusoidal Fourier embeddings, concatenated, and projected into a unified conditioning token $\mathbf{e}_{\text{act}}$.
  • Ego-Aligned Spatiotemporal Attention: Learnable BEV queries execute deformable spatial self-attention, followed by ego-pose-aligned deformable temporal cross-attention over the historical memory bank in $\Psi_{\mathcal{M}}$ to prevent inter-frame spatial drift.
  • Controllable 3D Volumetric Lifting: Cross-attention between the temporally fused queries and $\mathbf{e}_{\text{act}}$ injects controllable ego dynamics before an FFN outputs $\mathbf{B}_{+t}$, which channel-to-height prediction heads lift along the vertical axis into forecasted 3D semantic occupancy $\mathbf{Y}_{+t}^{\text{sem}}$ and 3D backward centripetal flow $\mathbf{V}_{+t}$.

Stage 3 — Closed-Loop Occupancy Planning & Refinement ($\Omega_{\text{Plan}}$)

  • Composite Volumetric Scoring: Command-guided candidate waypoints $\Xi_{+t}^*$ are evaluated via the occupancy cost functional $\mathcal{J}_{\text{occ}}$, which sums explicit agent-collision/proximity penalties, drivable-road boundary costs extracted from $\mathbf{Y}_{+t}^{\text{sem}}$, and a learned 2D dense cost map regressed from $\mathbf{B}_{+t}$.
  • Latent BEV Trajectory Refinement: The minimum-cost discrete waypoint $\xi_{+t}$ is encoded alongside the navigation directive into an ego query that cross-attends over $\mathbf{B}_{+t}$ and passes through MLPs to output the continuous refined waypoint $\xi_{\text{final}}$.
  • Autoregressive Rollout Feedback: During end-to-end planning, the selected trajectory is looped back directly as the active ego-action condition ($u_{t+1} \leftarrow \xi_{t+1}$) to condition $\Psi_{\mathcal{D}}$ for the next timestamp, enabling continuous, leakage-free 4D forecasting and planning.
